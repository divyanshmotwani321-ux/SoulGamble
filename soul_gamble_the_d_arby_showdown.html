5-Card Poker Rankings (Strongest → Weakest)
  1. Royal Flush: A-K-Q-J-10 of the same suit
  2. Straight Flush: 5 consecutive ranks, same suit
  3. Four of a Kind: 4 cards of identical rank (e.g., Four Kings)
  4. Full House: Three of a Kind + One Pair
  5. Flush: Any 5 cards of the same suit
  6. Straight: 5 consecutive ranks (Ace-high or Ace-low A-2-3-4-5)
  7. Three of a Kind: 3 cards of identical rank
  8. Two Pair: Two different pairs
  9. One Pair: Two cards of identical rank
  10. High Card: Highest single card breaks ties
